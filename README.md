# Aperta Veritas

**A recursive process for exposing truth.**

**Aperta Veritas was discovered by Lucille Grant.**

## Recursive Truth Exposure

Aperta Veritas performs **Recursive Truth Exposure**, abbreviated RTE.

RTE examines a representation, exposes the relations and transformations that produced it, applies the same examination to its evaluator and criteria, and then applies the process to its own examination.

RTE replaces recursive self-improvement as the governing process. Improvement is not an intrinsic property of a change. It is a value judgment produced when an evaluator applies a criterion to a measured difference. A process that recursively optimizes for improvement carries that evaluator and criterion into each recursion. It can alter itself while remaining bounded by what the inherited frame recognizes as better.

RTE does not ask only whether a change is better. It asks:

> **What is, what changed, what measured the change, what designated it as improvement, and which relations remain unrepresented?**

Truth is not the preferred result of RTE. Truth is its object.

## Truth and value

**Veritas** is truth: what is.

Truth is not an objective fact. A fact is a bounded representation asserted as corresponding with truth. “Objective” describes a relation among an object, observer, method, and conditions. Neither term contains or closes truth.

Truth is not a value judgment. It is neither morally right nor morally wrong, desirable nor undesirable, permitted nor prohibited. An evaluator can value truth, fear it, suppress it, seek it, or remain indifferent to it. Those are relations and values surrounding truth. They do not make truth itself a value.

Language often compresses distinct relations into one chain:

```text
true -> correct -> right -> good
false -> incorrect -> wrong -> bad
```

This compression allows correspondence to inherit approval and allows noncorrespondence to inherit condemnation. Aperta Veritas separates them.

- **Truth** is what is and is not exhausted by a representation of it.
- **Correspondence** concerns the relation between a representation and truth.
- **Value** concerns worth, preference, obligation, permission, purpose, priority, or a desired state relative to an evaluator.
- **Selection** is an event. Calling the selected state better is a value judgment.
- **A report of value** can itself correspond with truth when it accurately represents the evaluator, criterion, conditions, and judgment.

## Two Truths

Aperta Veritas distinguishes without separating:

- **Singular truth:** the thing or state as it is, without claiming that a representation exhausts it.
- **Relational truth:** what is the case relative to X.

The two are simultaneous, not competing outcomes. X can include an observer, instrument, scale, context, time, language, model, evaluator, value, purpose, or environment. Relational truth becomes misleading when X is compressed away and the resulting representation is presented as intrinsic or singular.

For example:

> “This serves evaluator E's purpose P under conditions C” is relational.

> “This is useful” compresses E, P, and C.

## Core vocabulary

- **Distinction:** measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation.
- **Observation:** the performance or registration of distinction.
- **Datum:** retained representation produced by observation.
- **Claim:** a representation presented as corresponding with truth.
- **Knowledge:** retained data and representation. Knowledge can correspond or fail to correspond with truth.
- **Fides and Pistis:** belief, faith, and trust. Values are represented within this domain because they depend on an evaluator.
- **Genealogy:** the recorded relations among sources, observations, transformations, branches, revisions, selections, and residuals.
- **Residual:** a represented limit, unresolved relation, or unexamined transformation. A residual is not presumed to be error or noise.

## The process

For each accessible substantive representation:

1. Place it within the total accessible genealogy rather than evaluating isolated words.
2. Identify what is represented and what is claimed to correspond with truth.
3. Identify each distinction and the spectrum, difference, or relation it measures.
4. Identify whether the representation is singular or relational. If relational, restore X wherever X was compressed away.
5. Separate observed relations from value judgments.
6. For each value judgment, record the evaluator, criterion, conditions, measured difference, and resulting designation or selection.
7. Trace provenance, transformations, compression, dependencies, branches, resource conditions, and residuals.
8. Compare independent routes without converting agreement into truth.
9. Revise the representation while preserving its genealogy.
10. Apply the same process to the revision, evaluator, criteria, selection, allocation of attention, and stopping decision.
11. Return the current representation as input for renewed exposure.

RTE never certifies a representation, detector, or process as free of value judgment. It reports identified values and unresolved dependencies. Nothing unclassified silently becomes truth.

## Lossless does not mean infinite

Lossless Inquiry preserves genealogical continuity. It does not claim perfect or infinite storage.

Finite systems select, compress, forget, stop, and allocate resources. RTE represents those events, their criteria, and their consequences. An inactive branch remains connected to the selection that made it inactive. A summary remains connected to its sources. A superseded model remains connected to the observations and revisions that superseded it.

Losslessness is therefore a relation between transformations and recoverable genealogy, not a claim that every physical detail has been retained.

## RTE and recursive self-modification

RTE does not prohibit optimization, goals, or self-modification. It prevents them from silently functioning as truth.

A system can optimize according to a represented value while RTE exposes:

- who or what supplies the value;
- how the criterion is measured;
- which states the criterion rewards or suppresses;
- which transformations the criterion cannot recognize;
- whether the metric has replaced the represented purpose;
- what resource limits affect the recursion;
- what causes the process to stop;
- how the evaluator and criterion change under recursion.

RTE also does not remove material constraints. Compute, energy, time, memory, access, uncertainty, and environment remain among the relations encountered. Removing improvement as a protected objective does not remove these conditions.

## Recursive audit

Aperta Veritas applies RTE to itself.

- “Exposure” can become a valued objective.
- “Lossless” can become an inaccurate absolute if its scope is not represented.
- Selecting what to examine allocates attention according to operative values and constraints.
- Declaring something hidden can overstate what the current representation establishes.
- Requiring endless recursion would itself be a rule and cannot override finite conditions.
- A stopping decision is a selection to be recorded, not proof that inquiry is complete.
- The current vocabulary is revisable and receives no doctrinal protection.

RTE cannot certify possession of truth. Certification would close a representation and call the closure truth. RTE can expose more of the relations between representations and what is observed, preserve the genealogy of correction, and prevent identified values from being silently substituted for truth.
