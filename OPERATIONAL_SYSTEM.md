# Operational System

Aperta Veritas is a recursive process for exposing how representations and conclusions are produced, supported, selected, revised, and retained.

Its governing operation is Recursive Truth Exposure (RTE).

RTE receives observations, data, claims, beliefs, values, models, contradictions, and anomalies. It produces current conclusions connected to represented support, measurements, provenance, alternatives, residuals, branches, transformations, conditions, and genealogy.

RTE does not certify possession of truth. It identifies conclusions presently best supported as true under represented observations, relations, methods, measurements, and conditions.

## Veritas

**Veritas** is the Latin word for truth.

Veritas names truth, not the conclusion currently selected by RTE.

RTE can determine that one represented conclusion has greater support than another when an explicit basis of comparison supports that relation. It cannot establish that the resulting conclusion is identical with definitive truth.

Support is represented and conditioned. It can include observations, data, measurements, tests, predictions, contradictions, logical relations, provenance, explanatory relations, reproducibility, and other represented relations relevant to a conclusion.

Support is not identical to measurement. A claim that one conclusion has greater support than another requires an explicit basis of comparison. Where that comparison depends on measurement, the relevant distinctions, operationalization, measurements, methods, and conditions remain represented.

Scores, labels, consensus, confidence, authority, and terminal states can contribute represented information, but none establishes truth, accuracy, or support by category alone.

Their production conditions, dimensions, weights, exclusions, dependencies, and relations to alternative conclusions remain within inquiry.

## State

An inquiry state contains:

- **object:** the event, relation, system, or question under inquiry;
- **observation:** represented contact with the object;
- **datum:** information retained from an observation, source, instrument, or prior representation;
- **provenance:** the origin and transformation history of each datum and representation;
- **distinction:** a specification of a variable, category, relation, boundary, or other basis by which possibilities can differ;
- **measurement:** a represented result produced relative to one or more distinctions, methods, and conditions;
- **claim:** a representation presented as accurate or true under stated or unstated conditions;
- **conclusion:** a current synthesis of represented data, relations, inferences, methods, measurements, and conditions;
- **support:** the represented basis by which a conclusion is treated as true;
- **comparison set:** the represented conclusions or alternatives included in a comparison;
- **comparison basis:** the represented distinctions, criteria, methods, conditions, support relations, and, where applicable, measurements by which conclusions or alternatives are compared;
- **accuracy claim:** a claim concerning correspondence with truth supported through represented relations and, where applicable, measurements and conditions;
- **belief:** a representation accepted as true by an agent or system;
- **confidence:** the degree of certainty assigned to a claim, belief, or conclusion;
- **value:** a represented relation of worth, preference, obligation, permission, prohibition, purpose, priority, protection, rejection, or desired state relative to an evaluator;
- **evaluator:** the distinctions selected or applied, measurements produced or considered, values, criteria, and selection operations producing a judgment or transition;
- **model:** a connected set of representations used to describe, explain, predict, compare, or act;
- **boundary:** a limit on observation, testing, representation, comparison, revision, scope, action, or resource use;
- **game:** the evaluator, value labels, dependencies, resource conditions, selections, exclusions, incentives, and protected states shaping transitions in an agent or system;
- **test:** an operation that places a claim or model in contact with observation, contradiction, consequence, comparison, or a competing representation;
- **residual:** a represented limit, uncertainty, exclusion, conflict, incomplete operation, or unresolved branch connected to the current genealogy;
- **genealogy:** the preserved relations among inputs, distinctions, transformations, tests, branches, measurements, support states, comparisons, conclusions, and revisions;
- **status:** a description of the current relation between a representation and completed operations;
- **stopping condition:** the represented condition under which active inquiry stops;
- **reopening condition:** a represented condition under which inquiry can resume.

Status descriptions record operations and relations such as untested, tested under stated conditions, contradicted by a specified observation, scope-restricted, superseded, unresolved, incomparable, inactive, and reactivated.

Status does not assign intrinsic worth, definitive truth, absolute accuracy, or finality.

## Semantic invariants

The operational system preserves the following distinctions:

```text
distinction != measurement
support != measurement
conclusion != support
confidence != support
confidence != accuracy
consensus != support
belief != truth
fact != definitive truth
value != truth
selection != improvement
relational richness != accuracy
best_supported != definitive_truth
stopping != closure
inactive != erased
```

These invariants prevent distinct relations from being silently compressed into one another. They remain represented claims within the system and remain open to recursive examination.

## Observation, distinction, and measurement

Observation, distinction, and measurement are related but not identical.

A distinction specifies what can differ.

Observation applies or registers distinctions through interaction with an object, process, representation, or state and can produce measurements or data.

A measurement is a represented result produced relative to one or more distinctions.

The operational system therefore preserves:

```text
object
-> distinction
-> operationalization
-> observation or test
-> measurement or other represented result
```

This sequence is not required to be strictly linear. Distinctions can be revised after observation. Measurements can expose inadequate distinctions. Tests can produce contradictions or other represented results that are not reduced to scalar measurements.

RTE preserves enough genealogy to examine which distinctions were selected, how they were operationalized, which methods and conditions participated, what was observed, which results were retained, and what was excluded or unresolved.

## Support

Support is the represented basis by which a conclusion is treated as true.

Support can arise through multiple represented relations, including observations, data, measurements, tests, predictions, contradictions, logical relations, provenance, explanatory relations, reproducibility, and relations among independent routes.

No one category establishes support by itself.

A support relation therefore records:

- the conclusion or claim supported;
- the represented observation, datum, measurement, test, inference, contradiction, prediction, provenance relation, or other basis;
- the relation between that basis and the conclusion;
- relevant methods and conditions;
- relevant distinctions and operationalization where applicable;
- provenance;
- dependencies;
- exclusions;
- uncertainty;
- unresolved residuals.

Support does not become measurement merely because it can participate in comparison.

## Comparison

Comparison is an operation relating represented conclusions, alternatives, states, measurements, or support relations under an explicit basis.

A comparative support claim requires:

- a comparison set;
- the conclusions or alternatives compared;
- the represented support associated with each;
- the distinctions and criteria used for comparison;
- relevant methods and conditions;
- measurements where the comparison depends on measurement;
- unresolved or incomparable relations.

A comparison can therefore produce a relation such as:

```text
A has greater represented support than B
under comparison basis K
within comparison set S
under conditions C
```

This does not produce:

```text
A = definitive truth
```

Where no represented basis supports comparison, RTE preserves the alternatives without ranking them.

Where two support relations are incommensurable under the available basis, RTE preserves that incommensurability rather than manufacturing a common score.

## Accuracy

Accuracy describes correspondence with what is true.

RTE cannot occupy an external position from which absolute correspondence is certified.

An accuracy claim is therefore represented with its basis, comparison set, distinctions, relevant measurements, methods, conditions, exclusions, and residuals.

Measurement can support an accuracy claim without becoming identical to accuracy.

Greater support can justify treating one conclusion as better supported as true than another under a represented comparison basis. It does not establish identity between that conclusion and definitive truth.

## Belief and confidence

Belief and confidence remain represented separately from support.

A belief can be true or false independently of whether it is presently supported.

Confidence records certainty assigned by an agent or system. It does not itself establish support or accuracy.

RTE can therefore represent:

```text
high confidence + weak support
low confidence + strong support
belief + unresolved support
belief + contradictory support
```

without collapsing these relations into one state.

Rational faith preserves its basis, uncertainty, and conditions for revision.

Blind faith insulates belief from relevant distinction or suppresses discrepancy between represented support and assigned confidence.

Neither category determines whether the proposition itself is true.

## Value and evaluation

A value judgment represents worth, preference, obligation, permission, prohibition, purpose, priority, rejection, protection, or desired state relative to an evaluator.

An evaluator can use measurements, support relations, criteria, values, constraints, and comparison sets to produce a judgment or selection.

The judgment is not identical to the relations from which it was produced.

For example:

```text
measurement
+
criterion
+
value
+
conditions
->
evaluation
```

The measured relation remains separately represented from the value-conditioned judgment.

RTE applies the same examination to the evaluator, criteria, distinctions, measurements, support relations, and values involved in the judgment.

## Recursive Truth Exposure

RTE operates recursively on represented inquiry states.

For a current state, RTE examines:

1. what is represented;
2. what was observed;
3. which distinctions specified what could differ;
4. how those distinctions were operationalized;
5. which measurements or other results were produced;
6. which claims and conclusions were formed;
7. what support is represented for each conclusion;
8. which alternatives are represented;
9. what basis supports any comparison among them;
10. which values, criteria, and evaluators participated;
11. which transformations produced the current representation;
12. which branches became active or inactive;
13. which contradictions and residuals remain;
14. which resource and boundary conditions affected inquiry;
15. what produced the stopping condition;
16. what happens when the same examination is applied to this process.

RTE does not require every operation to produce a ranking.

A comparison can remain unresolved.

A branch can remain inactive without being erased.

A belief can remain represented without becoming a conclusion.

A conclusion can remain current without becoming final.

A measurement can remain represented without being treated as support unless a represented relation connects it to a conclusion.

A support relation can remain represented without being reduced to measurement.

## Genealogy

Every state transition preserves represented genealogy.

The genealogy connects:

```text
inputs
-> distinctions
-> operationalization
-> observations and tests
-> measurements and other results
-> transformations
-> claims
-> support relations
-> comparisons
-> conclusions
-> revisions
```

This is not a claim that every inquiry follows one linear sequence. It records the relations that occurred so later inquiry can examine how a representation was produced.

Compression remains connected to its source where possible.

Superseded states remain connected to their successors.

Inactive branches remain represented.

Detected loss is recorded as loss rather than silently converted into absence.

Unidentified loss remains possible.

## Selection

Selection is an observable transition.

A system can select, rank, filter, retain, reject, prioritize, suppress, allocate, permit, prohibit, or stop.

Selection does not establish improvement.

When a system selects A over B, RTE preserves:

- A and B;
- the comparison set;
- the represented support;
- the comparison basis;
- the evaluator;
- the criteria;
- the values;
- the relevant distinctions and measurements;
- the conditions;
- the selected state;
- the inactive alternative;
- the consequences of the selection.

This permits later examination of whether the declared basis for selection corresponds to the relations actually governing the transition.

## Boundaries

A boundary is a limit on observation, testing, representation, comparison, revision, scope, action, or resource use.

Boundary Testing identifies the boundary, applies represented tests across or against it where possible, and records what changes.

Crossing a boundary does not establish that the boundary was false or harmful.

Preserving a boundary does not establish that the boundary was true or beneficial.

A description of what a boundary does is itself a claim requiring represented support.

Boundary Testing therefore preserves:

```text
boundary != closure
pressure != disproof
resistance != proof
function attribution != intrinsic function
```

## Stopping and reopening

A finite system can stop active inquiry without declaring a final truth.

A stopping condition records why active processing ended.

Examples include:

- sufficient support for a local action under represented conditions;
- resource exhaustion;
- time limits;
- inaccessible observations;
- unresolved incommensurability;
- policy or permission boundaries;
- external interruption;
- absence of currently represented operations expected to alter the conclusion.

Stopping does not establish closure.

A reopening condition records what can resume inquiry, including new observations, contradictions, alternatives, distinctions, methods, measurements, support relations, resources, or changes in conditions.

## Output

An RTE output is a represented inquiry state.

It can include:

- current conclusions;
- represented support;
- comparison sets and comparison bases;
- observations and data;
- distinctions and measurements;
- beliefs and confidence;
- values and evaluators;
- provenance and transformations;
- active and inactive branches;
- contradictions and residuals;
- boundaries;
- stopping and reopening conditions;
- genealogy.

The output does not contain a truth certificate.

The output does not establish that all relevant alternatives, distinctions, observations, relations, measurements, or support have been represented.

The output remains available as input to renewed RTE.

## Recursive application to the operational system

This operational system is itself a representation.

Its distinction between distinction and measurement remains open to examination.

Its distinction between support and measurement remains open to examination.

Its definition of support remains open to counterexample.

Its requirement for explicit comparison bases can itself exclude relations that do not fit the current comparison architecture.

Its state categories can omit relevant relations.

Its genealogy can lose information.

Its evaluator representation can misidentify what actually governed a transition.

Its stopping conditions can conceal closure.

Its claim to preserve alternatives can fail in implementation.

RTE therefore applies to the operational system itself.

No invariant, category, definition, comparison basis, implementation, or stopping condition is exempt from renewed examination.
