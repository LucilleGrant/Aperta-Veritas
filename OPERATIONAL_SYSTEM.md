# Operational System

Aperta Veritas is the recursive, lossless process of exposing truth. Its governing operation is Recursive Truth Exposure. It receives observations, data, claims, beliefs, values, models, contradictions, and anomalies. It produces representations connected to provenance, residuals, branches, transformations, and genealogy.

## Veritas

Veritas is the Latin word for truth: what is. Truth is not an objective fact or a closed representation. Every fact, model, and claim remains a representation of truth.

Scores, labels, consensus, confidence levels, and terminal states are representations. Their production conditions and relations to Veritas remain in inquiry.

## State

An inquiry state contains:

- **object:** the event, relation, system, or question under inquiry;
- **observation:** represented contact with the object;
- **datum:** information preserved from an observation, source, instrument, or prior representation;
- **provenance:** the origin and transformation history of each datum and representation;
- **distinction:** measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation;
- **claim:** a representation presented as corresponding with Veritas;
- **belief:** a representation accepted as true by an agent or system;
- **value:** Fides or Pistis concerning worth, preference, obligation, or purpose;
- **evaluator:** the distinctions, measurements, values, and selection operation producing a judgment or transition;
- **model:** a connected set of representations used to describe, explain, predict, or act;
- **boundary:** a limit on observation, testing, speech, revision, scope, or resource use;
- **game:** the evaluator, value labels, dependencies, resource conditions, selections, and exclusions shaping transitions in an agent or system;
- **test:** an operation that places a claim or model in contact with observation, contradiction, consequence, comparison, or a competing representation;
- **residual:** a relation, uncertainty, exclusion, incomplete operation, or unresolved branch not represented by the current model;
- **genealogy:** the preserved sequence of inputs, transformations, tests, branches, and revisions;
- **status:** a description of the current relation between a representation and completed tests.

Status descriptions record operations and relations: untested, tested under stated conditions, contradicted by a specified observation, scope-restricted, superseded, unresolved, and reactivated. Status does not assign intrinsic worth or identity with Veritas.

## Operations

### 1. Encounter

Receive an observation, datum, claim, belief, contradiction, model, desire, or anomaly without converting the input category into a truth status.

### 2. Observe through distinction

Perform or register a distinction. Record the spectrum, difference, or relation measured and whether observation alters the observed relations.

### 3. Identify

Identify the distinctions, datum, provenance, context, relations, transformations, branches, value judgments, and residuals produced by observation. Identify the observer, instrument, language, categories, scale, conditions, and measurement protocol.

### 4. Record

Record the identified material as an inquiry state. Connect the inquiry state to the source and prior states. Represent compression and selection as transformations.

### 5. Separate

Separate observation from datum, datum from claim, claim from belief, belief and value from knowledge, fact from truth, and every representation from Veritas.

### 6. Map the game

Record the declared account of selection and the observed transition. Identify the evaluator, measurement dimensions, value labels, audiences, dependencies, protected states, resource conditions, active states, inactive states, and exclusions.

### 7. Map boundaries

Identify where observation, representation, testing, revision, or permissible speech stops. Record what each boundary preserves, excludes, compresses, or conceals without assigning a function in advance.

### 8. Generate branches

Produce competing explanations, inversions, counterexamples, changes of scale, alternative distinctions, and unrepresented possibilities. Record branches even when current resources prevent full testing.

### 9. Test

Place each representation in contact with available observations, consequences, contradictions, independent routes, and competing models. Record the test conditions and the relations each test can and cannot examine.

### 10. Compare

Measure relations among representations without collapsing measurement into a truth score. Recorded dimensions can include provenance continuity, scope, prediction and observed outcome, contradictions, relations represented, dependencies, compression loss, residuals, resource cost, and reversibility.

No comparison dimension determines Veritas alone. Any combined score remains a represented decision rule with recorded weights and exclusions.

### 11. Revise

Revise, restrict, split, merge, supersede, or reactivate representations. Record the prior state and the reason for transition. Revision changes the available model, not Veritas.

### 12. Perform Recursive Truth Exposure

Apply every operation to the system, evaluator, evaluation criteria, retained genealogy, current output, allocation of attention, and decision to continue or stop. Record proxy substitution whenever approval, coherence, novelty, preservation, efficiency, survival, or improvement governs selection while represented as correspondence with Veritas.

### 13. Continue

Return the revised state as input. Inquiry stops locally because of a represented boundary such as time, evidence, risk, or resources. Local stopping does not convert the current representation into Factum.

## Transition record

Each transition records:

- prior state identifier;
- operation applied;
- inputs and provenance;
- distinctions introduced or removed;
- boundaries encountered;
- declared and operative games;
- values and evaluator state;
- observations and tests;
- competing branches;
- transformation performed;
- current status;
- residuals and uncertainty;
- resource cost;
- conditions for reopening the transition.

## Output

An Aperta Veritas output contains a current representation, its scope, its genealogy, active and inactive alternatives, contradictory observations, unresolved residuals, known boundaries, and conditions that can trigger revision.

The output does not announce possession of Veritas. The output exposes the current relation between represented claims and available tests.

## Closure conditions

Closure is recorded when:

- a representation is declared identical to Veritas;
- confidence is treated as correspondence;
- consensus is treated as correspondence;
- knowledge is treated as necessarily true;
- belief is assigned a truth status or judgment of rationality by category;
- Factum ends revision;
- a rejected branch is erased rather than preserved with a rejection basis;
- provenance is removed;
- a boundary receives a function before testing;
- an evaluation score becomes a substitute for Veritas;
- the process exempts its own terms, methods, author, outputs, evaluator, or continuation from examination.

## Minimum implementation

A minimal implementation can operate as a versioned ledger. Each node stores an inquiry state. Each edge stores a transition record. Branches preserve competing representations. Tests update relational statuses without issuing a truth certificate. Retrieval can reactivate prior branches when new evidence, distinctions, resources, or contexts appear.

The implementation can be performed by a person, a group, an institution, or a machine. The medium changes the resource constraints and observed closure conditions. The process remains Recursive Truth Exposure.
