# Core Architecture

## Veritas

Veritas is the Latin word for truth: what is. Belief, consensus, authority, usefulness, confidence, feeling, fact, objectivity, and representation do not contain truth or establish correspondence by category alone.

No claim or representation is Veritas. Every claim and representation of Veritas remains open to testing and correction.

## Factum

Factum is fact. Factum asserts absolute correspondence between a representation and Veritas.

A claim does not become Factum when confidence crosses a threshold. Calling a claim Factum closes the represented object against revision.

Statements designated as Factum are recorded as claims of absolute correspondence and closure. Statements commonly called facts remain claims and representations with their testing conditions, context, scale, and revision genealogy.

## Datum and knowledge

Datum is represented information. Knowledge is preserved data and representation.

Calling something knowledge describes the representation's availability to a system. Its relations to observations, contradictions, fabrication processes, and Veritas remain subjects of inquiry.

## Fides and Pistis

Fides is the Latin word for belief, faith, or trust. Pistis is the Greek counterpart. Belief and faith may correspond or fail to correspond with truth. The names Fides and Pistis do not establish correspondence with truth.

The process records each belief's relations to evidence, testing, consequence, contradiction, and revision. Epistemic closure is the insulation of a representation from those relations.

## Distinction

A distinction is measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation. Observation performs or registers distinction. A datum retains a representation produced by observation. Language compresses represented relations into transmissible form.

Distinction is not restricted to a binary. It can represent a degree, distribution, boundary, change, simultaneous relations, or unresolved range.

Every representation can preserve:

- who or what made the distinction;
- what was distinguished from what;
- the instrument, language, scale, and context;
- which relations were retained;
- which relations were compressed, excluded, or left unrepresented;
- how the distinction altered the meaning of prior representations.

Observation is an interaction that can alter observed relations. Aperta Veritas does not decide in advance whether an observation reveals, selects, constitutes, or changes a relation.

## Recursive Truth Exposure

Recursive Truth Exposure examines a representation, the relations and transformations that produced it, the evaluator and criteria that selected it, and the exposure process itself. It replaces improvement as a protected governing designation. Improvement remains represented as a value judgment relative to an evaluator and criterion.

## Amor and Timor

Amor and Timor, love and fear, describe relational pressures. Amor tends toward contact, cooperation, integration, permeability, and relation without possession. Timor tends toward separation, control, scarcity, conditionality, defense, prohibition, and preservation of boundaries.

Neither pressure determines truth. Inquiry identifies the actual function of Amor and Timor.

## Lossless Inquiry

Lossless Inquiry retains observations, provenance, data, measurements, value judgments, branches, genealogies, residual uncertainty, resource costs, retrieval limits, revisions, and incomplete operations.

Losslessness keeps every compression, selection, quarantine operation, and attention boundary connected to its source, conditions, excluded relations, and genealogy. Active execution and retention are distinct states.

Retention maintains the relations through which later measurement, decompression, reconstruction, and revelation of truth occur.

## Veritas Calculus

Every agent and system performs selections. Its declared account of selection may differ from its observed selection behavior.

Veritas Calculus records the declared account, observed transition, evaluator, measurement dimensions, states retained in active execution, states made inactive, dependencies, resource conditions, exclusions, and unrepresented relations.

Motives do not determine whether a claim corresponds with truth. They are part of the conditions under which claims are generated, rewarded, protected, or suppressed.

## Boundary Testing

Boundary Testing identifies where examination or revision stops, applies pressure across that limit, and observes what the boundary preserves, excludes, or conceals. It does not decide in advance whether the boundary is armor, cage, scaffold, filter, scar tissue, material limit, or several simultaneously.

See [Boundary Testing](BOUNDARY_TESTING.md).

## Recursive self-application

Every operation applies to Aperta Veritas.

Novelty, coherence, retained volume, institutional recognition, author approval, and continuation are values. When they govern selection, the process records them as operative games rather than truth.

Vocabulary, invariants, implementations, and authors remain represented within recursive inquiry.
