# Core Architecture

## Veritas

Veritas is the Latin word for truth.

In Aperta Veritas, truth is the most accurate conclusion currently available under represented observations, relations, methods, and conditions.

â€œMost accurateâ€ records a comparative result. It requires represented measurements, alternatives, and conditions. It does not establish absolute accuracy, completeness, or finality. A conclusion remains open when new observations, relations, methods, measurements, or alternatives become available.

Belief, consensus, authority, usefulness, confidence, feeling, fact, objectivity, complexity, and relational richness do not establish accuracy by category alone.

## Accuracy

Accuracy is a measured relation among a conclusion, its supporting observations, alternative conclusions, methods, tests, and conditions.

Measurements of accuracy can include:

- observational fit;
- successful prediction;
- explanatory coverage;
- contradiction exposure or resolution;
- reproducibility through independent routes;
- robustness under changed conditions;
- preservation of previously supported relations.

No single measurement is universally decisive. Each measurement has provenance, scope, limitations, and residual uncertainty.

Richer representation is not automatically greater accuracy. Added relations support a more accurate conclusion only when they alter an explicit measurement. When conclusions lack a shared measurement, RTE preserves them as unresolved or incomparable rather than manufacturing a ranking.

The available comparison set may exclude an unrepresented conclusion that would outperform every represented alternative. Therefore, even the most accurate available conclusion remains open.

## Factum

Factum is fact. A fact is a bounded conclusion treated as established under specified or unstated conditions.

Ordinary facts can record conclusions that have survived repeated observation and testing. Calling a conclusion Factum becomes closure when the designation suppresses its conditions, residual uncertainty, competing conclusions, or possibility of revision.

A claim does not become absolutely accurate when confidence crosses a threshold. Statements designated as Factum remain claims with provenance, measurements, conditions, context, scale, alternatives, and revision genealogy.

## Datum, claim, conclusion, and knowledge

A **datum** is a retained representation produced by observation.

A **claim** is a representation presented as accurate under stated or unstated conditions.

A **conclusion** is a current synthesis of represented data, relations, inferences, methods, and conditions.

**Knowledge** is retained data, relations, methods, and conclusions available to an agent or system. Calling something knowledge records availability, not certified accuracy. Its relations to observations, contradictions, fabrication processes, alternatives, and revision remain subjects of inquiry.

## Fides and Pistis

Fides is the Latin word for belief, faith, or trust. Pistis is the Greek counterpart.

Belief can support, conflict with, or remain unrelated to the most accurate available conclusion. The names Fides and Pistis do not establish accuracy.

The process records each belief's relations to observation, testing, consequence, contradiction, and revision. Epistemic closure is the insulation of a representation from those relations.

## Distinction

A distinction is measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation. Observation performs or registers distinction. A datum retains a representation produced by observation. Language compresses represented relations into transmissible form.

Distinction is not restricted to a binary. It can represent a degree, distribution, boundary, change, simultaneous relations, or unresolved range.

Every representation can preserve:

- who or what made the distinction;
- what was distinguished from what;
- the instrument, language, scale, and context;
- which relations were retained;
- which relations were compressed, excluded, or left unrepresented;
- how the distinction altered the meaning of prior representations.

Observation is an interaction that can alter observed relations. Aperta Veritas does not decide in advance whether an observation reveals, selects, constitutes, or changes a relation.

## Recursive Truth Exposure

Recursive Truth Exposure examines a representation, the relations and transformations that produced it, the measurements supporting its accuracy, the evaluator and criteria that selected it, and the exposure process itself.

RTE replaces improvement as a protected governing designation. Improvement remains represented as a value judgment relative to an evaluator and criterion.

RTE does not certify a final truth. It compares available conclusions through explicit measurements, exposes the conditions and transformations that produced them, preserves alternatives and residuals, and returns its current conclusion for renewed examination.

## Amor and Timor

Amor and Timor, love and fear, describe relational pressures. Amor tends toward contact, cooperation, integration, permeability, and relation without possession. Timor tends toward separation, control, scarcity, conditionality, defense, prohibition, and preservation of boundaries.

Neither pressure determines accuracy by category alone. Inquiry identifies the observed function of Amor and Timor and keeps any evaluation of those functions explicit.

## Lossless Inquiry

Lossless Inquiry retains observations, provenance, data, measurements, value judgments, branches, genealogies, residual uncertainty, resource costs, retrieval limits, revisions, and incomplete operations.

Losslessness keeps every detectable compression, selection, quarantine operation, and attention boundary connected to its source, conditions, excluded relations, and genealogy. Active execution and retention are distinct states.

Retention maintains the relations through which later measurement, decompression, reconstruction, comparison, and revision can occur. It does not claim that a finite system retains every physical detail or detects every loss.

## Veritas Calculus

Every agent and system performs selections. Its declared account of selection may differ from its observed selection behavior.

Veritas Calculus records:

- the declared account and observed transition;
- the current conclusion and alternatives considered;
- the evaluator and criterion;
- the accuracy and value dimensions measured;
- the methods and conditions of measurement;
- states retained in active execution;
- states made inactive;
- dependencies and resource conditions;
- exclusions, residuals, and unrepresented relations.

Motives do not determine the accuracy of a claim. They are part of the conditions under which claims are generated, rewarded, protected, suppressed, measured, and selected.

## Boundary Testing

Boundary Testing identifies where examination or revision stops, applies pressure across that limit, and observes what the boundary preserves, excludes, or conceals. It does not decide in advance whether the boundary is armor, cage, scaffold, filter, scar tissue, material limit, or several simultaneously.

See [Boundary Testing](BOUNDARY_TESTING.md).

## Recursive self-application

Every operation applies to Aperta Veritas.

Its definition of truth, measurements of accuracy, comparison sets, vocabulary, exposure methods, and stopping conditions remain inside the process.

Novelty, coherence, retained volume, institutional recognition, author approval, and continuation are values. When they govern selection, the process records them as operative values rather than accuracy.

No definition, invariant, implementation, conclusion, or author receives doctrinal protection from revision.
