# Changelog

All notable conceptual and structural changes will be recorded here.

## 0.3.0-draft, 2026-09-19

- Established Recursive Truth Exposure as the governing operation of Aperta Veritas.
- Identified improvement as an evaluator-dependent value judgment that bounds recursive self-improvement.
- Replaced the claim that truth is objective fact with the open formulation “Veritas is what is.”
- Defined fact as a bounded representation asserted as corresponding with truth.
- Defined objectivity as a relation among object, observer, method, and conditions rather than a property identical to truth.
- Reframed Aperta Veritas as the answer to closure of truth within facts, models, and representations.
- Restored the Two Truths structure as simultaneous singular and relational truth, with relational truth indexed to X.
- Defined distinction as measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation.
- Removed the binary requirement from distinction.
- Applied RTE to its own use of truth, exposure, losslessness, attention, recursion, and stopping.
- Licensed repository documentation under CC BY 4.0 with required attribution to Lucille Grant.
- Preserved earlier formulations below as genealogy rather than silently rewriting them.

## 0.2.0-draft, 2026-09-18

- Defined Aperta Veritas as the recursive, lossless process of revealing truth through observation, distinction, and measurement.
- Applied Aperta Veritas recursively to the repository's own language and operations.
- Recorded and removed the audit's missed question "What counts as winning?" because it reproduced value judgment in the voice of the process.
- Identified interpretation, improvement, usefulness, capability, rationality, correctness, success, failure, ranking, and optimization as possible carriers of value judgment.
- Removed value judgments from the inquiry state and operational criteria.
- Represented values as Fides or Pistis with evaluator, context, selection operation, exclusions, residuals, and genealogy.
- Replaced interpretation with recorded distinctions, measurements, relations, transformations, and claims.
- Replaced evaluative comparison dimensions with explicit measurements and their conditions.
- Replaced Veritas Calculus questions about winning, loss, and objectives with records of declared accounts, observed transitions, evaluator state, active and inactive branches, dependencies, resource conditions, exclusions, and residuals.
- Replaced failure conditions with observed closure conditions.
- Separated active execution from genealogical retention so resource-conditioned selection does not erase inactive branches.
- Added the RSI claim that value-directed successor selection carries an evaluator's exclusions forward and constrains recursive revision of the evaluator.
- Applied recursion to evaluators, value judgments, selection operations, stopping decisions, and the current account of Aperta Veritas.

## 0.1.0-draft, 2026-09-18

- Named the integrated system **Aperta Veritas**.
- Established Veritas as the Latin term for truth while keeping every claim and representation of truth open to testing and correction.
- Integrated the Distinction Principle, Amor/Timor, and Lossless Inquiry.
- Defined Veritas Calculus for identifying declared and operative games.
- Defined Boundary Testing as a neutral examination of epistemic limits.
- Required recursive application of every operation to the system itself.
- Separated truth, fact, knowledge, data, claims, and belief.
- Defined Factum as a claim of absolute correspondence and recorded its closure operation.
- Defined Fides and Pistis as belief or faith and represented judgments of rationality as values rather than truth statuses.
- Added AI epistemic alignment as an application and separated truth from human values.
- Added the coin toss as a worked example of distinction, measurement failure, forced classification, and protocol-defined outcomes.
- Adopted Amor/Timor as the formal terminology while retaining love and fear as its plain-English gloss.
- Formalized state, operations, transitions, outputs, and closure conditions as an operational system.
- Produced the first whitepaper through recursive application of the operational system.
- Added a preserved audit of the whitepaper's distinctions, boundary tests, revisions, and residuals.
- Removed metaphysical realism as an untested premise of the Distinction Principle.
- Applied a language audit to the repository, represented values as Fides and Pistis, and removed value judgments from the inquiry operations.
- Separated observation, measurement, identification, recording, and retention into distinct operations.
