### Truth and support correction

Further Recursive Truth Exposure showed that the initial 0.3.1 revision still contained a compression:

```text
best supported as true
->
truth
```

The intermediate definition of truth as "the most accurate conclusion currently available" was therefore superseded within draft 0.3.1.

- Removed the formulation that defined truth as the most accurate conclusion currently available.
- Defined truth as **what is true**.
- Distinguished truth from conclusions presently supported as true.
- Defined RTE as identifying conclusions presently best supported as true under represented observations, relations, methods, conditions, and other represented support.
- Established that RTE cannot certify that its presently best-supported conclusion is identical with definitive truth.
- Did not replace the rejected formulation with the opposite metaphysical claim that definitive truth is inaccessible or nonexistent.
- Preserved truth as the referent of truth claims without claiming that RTE possesses or certifies it.
- Clarified that a conclusion's represented support can change without establishing that truth itself changed.
- Preserved the support genealogy by which a conclusion is treated as true.
- Established:

```text
conclusion != support
best_supported != definitive_truth
```

- Clarified that absence of a better represented alternative cannot establish truth because relevant alternatives, observations, relations, distinctions, methods, or conditions can remain outside the represented possibility space.
- Replaced language implying that RTE produces or possesses truth with language describing conclusions presently supported as true.
- Preserved superseded truth formulations in the audit and changelog rather than rewriting them to appear consistent with the correction.

Recursive examination exposed another compression:

Earlier formulations had defined distinction as measurement of a spectrum of possibility.

That formulation was superseded.

- Defined a distinction as specifying what can differ.
- Allowed distinctions to specify variables, categories, relations, boundaries, or other bases of differentiation.
- Defined measurement primarily as a represented result produced relative to one or more distinctions.
- Established:

```text
distinction != measurement
```

- Represented operationalization separately from distinction.
- Represented measurement separately from operationalization.
- Clarified that observation can apply or register distinctions and can produce measurements or other data.
- Clarified that distinction need not be binary.
- Clarified that measurement need not be quantitative.
- Allowed measurements to be quantitative, qualitative, categorical, relational, comparative, bounded, or unresolved.
- Clarified that measurement can depend on distinctions, operationalization, object or process, method, instrument, and conditions.
- Clarified that different distinctions can produce different measurements of the same object without necessarily conflicting.
- Preserved distinction itself as an object of recursive examination.
- Preserved measurement itself as an object of recursive examination.
- Recorded ordinary technical usage of "measurement" for both procedure and result as a residual while using the term primarily for represented results in the current framework.
- Replaced the earlier compressed genealogy with a nonmandatory structure that can include:

```text

-> operationalization
-> observation or test
-> measurement or other represented result
```

- Clarified that comparison does not require a common scalar metric.
- Clarified that comparison does not require a shared measurement in every case.
- Preserved measurements explicitly where the comparison actually depends on measurement.

- Preserved incommensurability rather than forcing incomparable relations into a scalar score.
- Clarified that the existence of a shared measurement does not by itself establish that a proposed comparison is defensible.
- Clarified that the absence of a shared measurement does not by itself establish that no comparison is possible.
- Separated comparison from measurement while preserving their possible relation.
