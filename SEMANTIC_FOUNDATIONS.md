# Semantic Foundations

Truth, fact, knowledge, belief, and data are routinely compressed into one epistemic hierarchy. The compression allows confidence, consensus, authority, or institutional acceptance to masquerade as truth.

Aperta Veritas separates the terms before applying any method.

## Veritas and Factum

Veritas is the Latin word for truth: what is.

Truth is not an objective fact. Fact is a bounded representation asserted as corresponding with truth. Objectivity describes relations among an object, observer, method, and conditions. Neither closes or contains Veritas.

Factum is fact. Factum asserts absolute correspondence between a representation and Veritas. Factum closes the represented object against further revision.

The statement "this is a fact" remains a claim. No label can establish absolute correspondence between a representation and truth.

> **Truth, fact, and belief are distinct. Representations of truth remain open. Factum closes a representation.**

## Datum and knowledge

Datum is represented information. Knowledge is data preserved and organized by an agent or system.

Knowledge can contain observations, contradictions, transformations, fabrications, and unresolved branches. The designation of knowledge records availability to an agent or system rather than correspondence with truth.

## Fides and Pistis

Fides is the Latin word for belief, faith, or trust. Pistis is the Greek counterpart. Belief is the acceptance of a representation as true.

A belief may correspond with truth. The act of believing does not produce that correspondence. Sincerity, confidence, loyalty, repetition, and consensus can alter the persistence and distribution of belief without establishing correspondence.

Inquiry records how belief and faith respond to evidence, testing, consequence, contradiction, and revision. Insulation from those relations is recorded as epistemic closure. Neither belief nor faith determines truth by name alone.

Testing produces further representations, not possession of Veritas. Further testing remains available for every representation.

## Aperta

Aperta means open or uncovered. Claims and representations of Veritas remain within Recursive Truth Exposure. Testing produces measurements of specified relations under stated conditions.

> **Every claim of truth remains open.**

Aperta Veritas applies this openness recursively. Its own semantics, methods, and conclusions cannot claim immunity from the process they impose on other representations.
