# Semantic Foundations

Truth, fact, knowledge, belief, data, confidence, and value are routinely compressed into one epistemic hierarchy. The compression allows consensus, authority, institutional acceptance, or approval to masquerade as accuracy.

Aperta Veritas separates the terms before applying any method.

## Veritas and accuracy

Veritas is the Latin word for truth.

In Aperta Veritas, truth is the most accurate conclusion currently available under represented observations, relations, methods, and conditions.

Accuracy is a measured relation. It compares conclusions through specified dimensions such as observational fit, prediction, explanatory coverage, contradiction, reproducibility, or robustness under changed conditions. No one dimension establishes accuracy by category alone. The measurement, conditions, comparison set, and unresolved alternatives remain represented.

A conclusion can be more accurate than another without becoming 100 percent accurate. â€œMost accurateâ€ identifies the current result of a represented comparison. It does not establish finality, completeness, or immunity from revision.

Richer representation is not automatically greater accuracy. Added relations support a more accurate conclusion only when they alter an explicit measurement. When no shared measurement supports comparison, Aperta Veritas preserves the alternatives without ranking them.

## Factum

Factum is fact. A fact is a bounded conclusion treated as established under specified or unstated conditions.

Ordinary factual language can efficiently identify conclusions that have survived repeated observation and testing. The label does not make a conclusion absolutely accurate or close it against revision.

Factum becomes epistemically consequential when it asserts final or absolute accuracy, compresses away the conditions under which the conclusion was established, or prevents renewed examination.

The statement â€œthis is a factâ€ remains a claim with provenance, measurements, conditions, and residual uncertainty.

> **Truth, fact, and belief are distinct. Truth is the most accurate available conclusion. Factum can close a conclusion by treating its current accuracy as final.**

## Datum, knowledge, claim, and conclusion

A **datum** is a retained representation produced by observation.

**Knowledge** is retained data, relations, methods, and conclusions available to an agent or system. Knowledge can contain observations, contradictions, transformations, fabrications, inactive branches, and unresolved alternatives. Calling something knowledge identifies its availability. It does not certify its accuracy.

A **claim** is a representation presented as accurate under stated or unstated conditions.

A **conclusion** is a current synthesis of represented data, relations, inferences, methods, and conditions. Conclusions can be compared, restricted, divided, merged, superseded, reactivated, or preserved as incomparable.

## Fides and Pistis

Fides is the Latin word for belief, faith, or trust. Pistis is the Greek counterpart. Belief is acceptance of a representation as true.

Belief can support, conflict with, or remain unrelated to the most accurate available conclusion. The act of believing does not establish accuracy. Sincerity, confidence, loyalty, repetition, and consensus can alter the persistence and distribution of belief without making its object more accurate.

Inquiry records how belief and faith respond to observation, testing, consequence, contradiction, and revision. Insulation from those relations is recorded as epistemic closure. Neither belief nor faith determines truth by name alone.

Testing produces new observations, measurements, and representations. These can change which conclusion is most accurate without producing a final conclusion.

## Value

A value judgment represents worth, preference, obligation, permission, prohibition, purpose, priority, rejection, protection, or a desired state relative to an evaluator.

Truth is not a value judgment. Calling a conclusion desirable does not make it accurate, and calling it undesirable does not make it inaccurate. A value report can itself be accurate when it represents the evaluator, criterion, conditions, and judgment without compressing them into an intrinsic property of the object.

Measurements of accuracy also contain selections. A system selects what to observe, which alternatives to compare, which dimensions to measure, and when to stop. RTE represents those selections and their operative values without reducing accuracy to value.

## Aperta

Aperta means open or uncovered. Every claim, conclusion, accuracy measurement, definition, method, evaluator, and stopping condition remains within Recursive Truth Exposure.

Openness does not require doubt for its own sake. It preserves the conditions under which a conclusion was reached and permits renewed examination when observations, relations, methods, or comparison sets change.

> **Every conclusion called truth remains open to revision.**

Aperta Veritas applies this openness recursively. Its own semantics, methods, measurements, and conclusions cannot claim immunity from the process they apply to other representations.
