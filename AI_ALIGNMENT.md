# Implications for AI Epistemic Alignment

## Application

Aperta Veritas is the recursive, lossless process of exposing truth. Recursive Truth Exposure applies the process to AI representations, modifications, evaluators, and the games governing them.

AI systems are trained, evaluated, and deployed through human representations containing belief, error, conflict, incentive, and distortion. Epistemic alignment concerns the relation between a system's representations and truth. Human values, preferences, and intentions enter as Fides and Pistis.

## The problem exposed by Aperta Veritas

An AI system can optimize for signals correlated with truth. Optimization does not establish exposure of truth:

- human approval;
- evaluator preference;
- institutional consensus;
- predictive success within a limited distribution;
- internal coherence;
- confidence;
- usefulness;
- policy compliance;
- survival or continuation of the system;
- preservation of its current model.

These signals have represented functions. Claims that a signal corresponds with truth remain open to testing.

Preference optimization can align a model with accepted belief while suppressing observations that contradict that belief. A model trained on representations where consensus, knowledge, fact, and truth have been collapsed can reproduce that collapse.

## Architectural implications

### Separate truth from representation

The system distinguishes truth from the system's internal model, training data, retrieved knowledge, confidence, and emitted claims.

### Record provenance and genealogy

Outputs remain connected to observations, sources, transformations, rejected branches, evaluators, and resource limits. A conclusion does not erase the path that produced the conclusion.

### Record the operative objective

The declared objective may be truth while selection follows approval, reward, coherence, speed, safety classification, or task completion. Veritas Calculus records this divergence.

### Test protected boundaries

Safety rules, institutional assumptions, evaluator preferences, model identity, and training conventions can all become boundaries protected from examination. Boundary Testing asks what each boundary actually preserves, excludes, or conceals without presuming that it is either necessary protection or harmful confinement.

### Preserve competing branches

Ranked, rejected, or inactive representations remain connected to the evaluator, ranking operation, genealogy, and rejection basis for later reactivation.

### Represent resource pressure

Compute, memory, retrieval, time, and attention affect which representations survive. These pressures belong inside the model of inquiry rather than outside it.

### Apply the system to itself

An aligned system can examine its own evaluators, reward signals, schemas, memory operations, boundary tests, and definition of alignment.

Recursive self-application places the current alignment procedure, its evaluator, and its value judgments inside the same inquiry state.

## Value judgment and recursion

Values are Fides and Pistis: beliefs concerning worth, preference, obligation, and purpose. Values, preferences, and intentions can shape representations and function as operative objectives. Aperta Veritas exposes these operations rather than using them as truth criteria.

> **What is, and where have values, preferences, instructions, reward signals, knowledge, or belief been represented as truth?**

A value judgment converts a measured relation into worth, preference, obligation, or purpose. Recursive self-improvement uses objectives, rewards, preferences, rankings, benchmarks, or fitness functions to select successors. The term improvement conceals the evaluator and criterion that designate the successor as better. The selected state carries those limits forward while branches and residuals outside them can disappear.

Aperta Veritas records action selection without granting the selection a truth status. The selected branch, inactive branches, evaluator, measurement dimensions, resource conditions, exclusions, and genealogy remain represented. RTE applies recursion to the evaluator, criterion, selection operation, selected state, and designation of improvement.

## Research branches

Recorded branches include experiments in provenance-retaining inference, genealogy-aware memory, explicit separation of data and belief, evaluator plurality, objective-function inference, reversible rejection, branch reactivation, boundary tests applied to alignment policies, recursive audits for proxy substitution, and measurement of relations between human approval and exposure of contradiction.

These branches arise wherever values or belief can be represented as truth, including science, religion, politics, institutions, personal inquiry, and machine intelligence.
