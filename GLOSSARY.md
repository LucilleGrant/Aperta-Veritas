# Glossary

## Aperta

Open or uncovered. Aperta describes inquiry and representation that remain available for testing and revision.

## Amor

Love, affection, desire, or attachment. In Aperta Veritas, Amor names relational pressure toward contact, cooperation, integration, permeability, and relation without possession. Amor is not a moral verdict and does not determine truth.

## Belief

Acceptance of a representation as true. Belief may correspond with truth, but belief does not create or certify truth. See Fides and Pistis.

## Boundary

A represented limit on contact, action, speech, measurement, revision, scope, or resource use. A boundary may protect, confine, compress, allocate, conceal, or perform several functions simultaneously.

## Boundary Testing

The operation of locating closure, applying pressure across it, and identifying what the boundary preserves, excludes, or conceals without assigning its function in advance.

## Claim

A representation presented as corresponding with truth. A claim may be tested, contradicted by a specified observation, restricted in scope, split, superseded, or reactivated.

## Compression

The reduction of represented relations into a smaller form. Compression records some relations and excludes others; its conditions and genealogy remain connected to the compressed representation.

## Convergence

Agreement produced through multiple routes. Convergence may provide evidence, but it does not equal Veritas. The routes may share dependencies, incentives, distinctions, or errors.

## Datum

Represented information. A datum is not certified as true by being represented.

## Distinction

Measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation. A distinction can represent a degree, distribution, boundary, change, simultaneous relations, or unresolved range.

## Factum

Fact. Factum asserts absolute correspondence between a representation and Veritas and closes the represented object against revision. Aperta Veritas records Factum as a claim of correspondence and closure.

## Fides

The Latin word for belief, faith, or trust. Fides can correspond or fail to correspond with truth. Its relations to evidence, testing, contradiction, and revision are recorded without a judgment of rationality.

## Fidelity

The degree and manner in which a representation preserves relevant relations of what it attempts to represent. Fidelity remains open to testing.

## Game

The evaluator, value labels, dependencies, resource conditions, selections, exclusions, and protected states shaping transitions in an agent or system.

## Knowledge

Retained data and representation. The name records availability to an agent or system rather than correspondence with truth.

## Lie

A distortion represented as truth with awareness of the distortion.

## Lossless Inquiry

The discipline of preserving provenance, genealogy, alternatives, residuals, and transformations so that revision does not masquerade as erasure. Lossless Inquiry is a supporting architecture within Aperta Veritas.

## Pistis

Greek for belief or faith. Pistis can correspond or fail to correspond with truth. Its relations to evidence, testing, contradiction, and revision are recorded without a judgment of rationality.

## Residual

A relation, distinction, uncertainty, or branch outside the current representation and connected to its genealogy.

## Recursive Truth Exposure

The governing operation of Aperta Veritas. RTE exposes a representation, its relations and transformations, its evaluator and criteria, its selections and residuals, and then applies the same process to the exposure itself.

## Timor

Fear, dread, apprehension, alarm, or anxiety. In Aperta Veritas, Timor names relational pressure toward separation, control, scarcity, conditionality, defense, prohibition, and preservation of boundaries. Timor is not a moral verdict and does not determine truth.

## Truth

What is. Truth is not an objective fact, a value judgment, or a closed representation. Consensus, authority, identity, feeling, usefulness, confidence, belief, fact, and representation do not contain truth or establish correspondence by category alone. See Veritas.

## Veritas

The Latin word for truth: what is. Every claim and representation of truth remains open to Recursive Truth Exposure.

## Value

Fides or Pistis concerning worth, preference, obligation, or purpose. A value can govern attention, selection, retention, action, and revision. Its operation and genealogy remain represented; its status as value does not establish correspondence with Veritas.

## Value judgment

An operation that converts a measured relation into worth, preference, obligation, or purpose. Aperta Veritas records the evaluator, distinctions, measurement, conditions, exclusions, selected branch, inactive branches, residuals, and genealogy of the operation.

## Veritas Calculus

The analysis of what an agent or system declares it is pursuing, what it preserves under pressure, and which game governs its representations.
