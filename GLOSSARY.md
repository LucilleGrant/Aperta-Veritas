# Glossary

## Accuracy

A measured relation among a conclusion, its supporting observations, alternative conclusions, methods, tests, and conditions. Accuracy is comparative and scoped. It is not an intrinsic property, value judgment, or certificate of finality.

## Aperta

Open or uncovered. Aperta describes inquiry, conclusions, measurements, and representations that remain available for testing and revision.

## Amor

Love, affection, desire, or attachment. In Aperta Veritas, Amor names relational pressure toward contact, cooperation, integration, permeability, and relation without possession. Amor is not a moral verdict and does not determine accuracy by category alone.

## Belief

Acceptance of a representation as true. Belief can support, conflict with, or remain unrelated to the most accurate available conclusion. Belief does not create or certify accuracy. See Fides and Pistis.

## Boundary

A represented limit on contact, action, speech, measurement, revision, scope, or resource use. A boundary may protect, confine, compress, allocate, conceal, or perform several functions simultaneously.

## Boundary Testing

The operation of locating closure, applying pressure across it, and identifying what the boundary preserves, excludes, or conceals without assigning its function in advance.

## Claim

A representation presented as accurate under stated or unstated conditions. A claim may be tested, contradicted by a specified observation, restricted in scope, split, merged, superseded, or reactivated.

## Comparison set

The represented conclusions or alternatives included in an accuracy comparison. The most accurate conclusion within a comparison set may be less accurate than an excluded or unrepresented alternative.

## Compression

The reduction of represented relations into a smaller form. Compression retains some relations and excludes others. Its conditions and genealogy remain connected to the compressed representation when recoverable.

## Conclusion

A current synthesis of represented data, relations, inferences, methods, and conditions. A conclusion can be revised, restricted, divided, merged, superseded, reactivated, or preserved as incomparable with another conclusion.

## Convergence

Agreement produced through multiple routes. Convergence can support an accuracy measurement, but agreement does not establish accuracy by category alone. The routes may share dependencies, incentives, distinctions, or errors.

## Datum

A retained representation produced by observation. A datum is not certified as accurate merely by being represented or retained.

## Distinction

Measurement of a spectrum of possibility that produces a represented difference or relation. A distinction can represent a degree, distribution, boundary, change, simultaneous relations, or unresolved range.

## Factum

Fact. A fact is a bounded conclusion treated as established under specified or unstated conditions. Factum becomes closure when the designation suppresses conditions, alternatives, residual uncertainty, or renewed examination.

## Fides

The Latin word for belief, faith, or trust. Fides can support, conflict with, or remain unrelated to the most accurate available conclusion. Its relations to observation, testing, contradiction, and revision are recorded without a judgment of rationality.

## Fidelity

The degree and manner in which a representation preserves the relations selected as relevant to what it represents. Fidelity depends on represented criteria and remains open to testing.

## Game

The evaluator, value labels, dependencies, resource conditions, selections, exclusions, and protected states shaping transitions in an agent or system.

## Knowledge

Retained data, relations, methods, and conclusions available to an agent or system. The name records availability rather than certified accuracy.

## Lie

A representation presented as accurate by an agent that represents it as inaccurate. This definition concerns the represented relation between the agent, claim, and known contradiction. It does not presume access to the agent's unrepresented internal state.

## Lossless Inquiry

The discipline of preserving provenance, genealogy, alternatives, residuals, and transformations so that revision does not masquerade as erasure. Lossless Inquiry describes recoverable genealogical continuity, not perfect or infinite retention.

## Objectivity

Methods and relations intended to reduce dependence on a particular observer. Objectivity can support accuracy through public methods, reproducible measurements, independent routes, explicit instruments, and stated conditions. It does not certify final truth.

## Observation

The performance or registration of distinction. Observation is an interaction that can reveal, select, constitute, or alter represented relations. Aperta Veritas does not decide its function in advance.

## Pistis

The Greek word for belief or faith. Pistis can support, conflict with, or remain unrelated to the most accurate available conclusion. Its relations to observation, testing, contradiction, and revision are recorded without a judgment of rationality.

## Relational richness

The quantity, diversity, or detail of relations included in a representation. Relational richness is not identical to accuracy. Added relations support greater accuracy only when they alter an explicit measurement.

## Residual

A represented limit, unresolved relation, known exclusion, uncertainty, inactive branch, or unexamined transformation connected to the current genealogy. A residual is not presumed to be error or noise.

## Recursive Truth Exposure

The governing operation of Aperta Veritas. RTE exposes a representation, the observations, relations, transformations, and measurements supporting it, its evaluator and criteria, its selections and residuals, and then applies the same process to the exposure itself.

## Timor

Fear, dread, apprehension, alarm, or anxiety. In Aperta Veritas, Timor names relational pressure toward separation, control, scarcity, conditionality, defense, prohibition, and preservation of boundaries. Timor is not a moral verdict and does not determine accuracy by category alone.

## Truth

The most accurate conclusion currently available under represented observations, relations, methods, and conditions. Truth is comparative, conditioned, and open to revision. It never becomes 100 percent complete or final.

## Value

Fides or Pistis concerning worth, preference, obligation, permission, purpose, priority, protection, rejection, or a desired state. A value can govern attention, selection, retention, action, measurement, and revision. Its operation and genealogy remain represented. Its status as value does not establish accuracy.

## Value judgment

An operation that converts a measured relation into worth, preference, obligation, permission, purpose, priority, protection, rejection, or a desired state. Aperta Veritas records the evaluator, distinctions, measurement, conditions, exclusions, selected branch, inactive branches, residuals, and genealogy of the operation.

## Veritas

The Latin word for truth. In Aperta Veritas, Veritas names the most accurate conclusion currently available under represented observations, relations, methods, and conditions. Every use of the name remains open to Recursive Truth Exposure.

## Veritas Calculus

The analysis of what an agent or system declares it is pursuing, what it measures, what it selects and preserves under pressure, which alternatives it excludes, and which values govern its representations and transitions.
