# Aperta Veritas: Two-Page Research Brief

**Recursive Truth Exposure as an inspectable alternative to evaluator-only self-critique**

Lucille Grant  
Research-outreach draft, 2026

## Status and scope

Aperta Veritas (AV) is an open framework for inquiry governed by Recursive Truth Exposure (RTE). RTE recursively examines how representations, conclusions, candidate explanations, and possible inquiry operations are produced and related. It does **not** certify possession of truth. It identifies conclusions presently supported as true under represented observations, relations, methods, measurements where applicable, conditions, and support claims, while preserving the genealogy and limits of that support.

This brief proposes a research program. It reports no completed comparative experiment and makes no validated performance claim.

## Problem

Many recursive or self-correcting systems focus on a loop of the form:

```text
answer -> critique under an evaluator -> revision -> reevaluation
```

That loop can improve an output under represented criteria. It does not by itself expose why those criteria govern the evaluation; how the candidate set was generated; which alternatives never became representable; which inquiries were generated but not allocated resources; or whether stopping was converted into epistemic closure.

AV treats these as different operations:

```text
generation != allocation
allocation != evaluation
evaluation != generation
```

An evaluator can rank only represented candidates. A generator can make an inquiry possible without causing it to receive resources. An allocator can activate one inquiry without establishing that inquiry as true, supported, or preferable on every basis.

## Core proposal

RTE represents and reexamines, where available: observations, data, provenance, distinctions, operationalizations, measurements, conclusions, beliefs, confidence, acceptance bases, inquiry bases, support claims, generators, allocation bases, priorities, resource allocations, evaluators, criteria, values, comparison bases, alternatives, residuals, transformations, stopping conditions, reopening conditions, and genealogy.

Several separations are central:

```text
basis != support
acceptance_basis != support
support_claim != truth
confidence != support
selection != improvement
failure_to_establish != disproof
not_allocated != rejected
open_inquiry != active_inquiry
stopping != closure
evaluated_candidates != exhaustive_possibility_space
best_supported != definitive_truth
```

RTE permits a current conclusion while keeping its evaluator, generator, allocator, and support claims open to revision. An output may remain conditional, unresolved, branched, or unranked when no represented comparison basis supports a ranking.

## Open Genealogy and Convergent Inquiry

**Open Genealogy** retains recoverable relations among prior observations, transformations, exclusions, inactive branches, and superseded conclusions so later distinctions can recontextualize them without rewriting their historical states.

**Convergent Inquiry** generates and examines possible continuations of unresolved inquiry: new distinctions, hypotheses, relations, tests, operationalizations, measurements, comparison bases, and support claims. “Convergent” does not imply mathematical convergence, monotonic improvement, or guaranteed approach to truth.

In a finite system, retention, generation, and examination remain resource-conditioned. RTE therefore represents allocation rather than assuming neutral or unlimited attention.

## Researchable claim

The defensible empirical question is not “Does RTE find truth?” It is:

> Under matched models, tools, resource ceilings, task inputs, and stopping conditions, does an RTE treatment expose relevant distinctions, candidates, errors, allocation events, or recontextualizations that a baseline self-critique loop does not expose?

This requires an explicit relevance rubric, blinded verification where practical, held-out tasks, complete resource accounting, preregistered exclusions, and reporting of negative results. More candidates, more text, or deeper recursion are not benefits by themselves.

## Proposed comparison

- **Baseline self-critique:** generate an answer, critique it under a fixed task rubric, revise, and repeat.
- **RTE condition:** use the same model, task, tools, budget, sampling settings, and stopping envelope, but require structured representation and recursive examination of generation, allocation, evaluation, genealogy, and reopening conditions.

Primary outcomes are missing-distinction recovery, relevant-candidate recovery, error discovery, allocation-boundary exposure, and unsupported-closure rate. Secondary outcomes include precision, latency, compute, tokens, storage, verifier labor, recursion depth, and sensitivity to model and prompt changes.

The protocol includes adversarial tasks in which the evaluator imports a criterion not authorized by the task, and shared-boundary tasks in which neither condition can recover the needed information. Any advantage that disappears under resource equalization is reported as a resource effect. Any advantage driven by AV vocabulary is benchmark contamination.

## Falsifiability and limits

Results would count against the tested RTE treatment if it fails registered primary outcomes on held-out tasks; improves recall only by candidate flooding; reproduces the baseline after compute and retained-volume equalization; labels evaluator revision as truth exposure without an independent basis; records genealogy that is never used; or merely relocates a fixed boundary into a larger prompt or schema.

No finite representation can establish that every relevant distinction, candidate, support relation, or loss has been represented. RTE can itself impose vocabulary, schema, retention, and allocation biases. Its categories and procedures remain objects of RTE.
