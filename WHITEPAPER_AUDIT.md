## Boundary tests

### Can Aperta Veritas certify itself?

No operation inside the system converts the current system description into Veritas. The system description remains a representation subject to the same operations as every other representation.

### Can openness become a protected doctrine?

Yes. A demand for openness can conceal resource limits, privacy, safety, or material constraints. Boundary Testing therefore examines the function of closure in context rather than assuming every boundary is error.

### How is losslessness represented under finite resources?

Active execution and retention are separate states. Lossless Inquiry connects transformations, provenance, inactive branches, and residuals to their genealogy under represented resource conditions. Compression remains connected to its source and exclusions.

### Can comparison become a truth score?

Yes. Provenance, prediction, coherence, explanatory coverage, contradiction load, and other comparison dimensions can become proxies for Veritas. The operational system preserves the dimensions, weights, exclusions, and residuals of any comparison rule.

### Does rejecting Factum prevent practical decisions?

No. Inquiry can stop locally under represented constraints and act on a current model. Local stopping does not declare the model identical to Veritas. The relation between open inquiry and time-sensitive action remains a subject for further development.
